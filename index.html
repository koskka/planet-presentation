Activation Rate

The share of new users who reach a product's real value moment — not just sign up.

Completion Rate

The share of users who finish the onboarding steps or checklist — whether or not they felt any value.

Completion and what actually happens after are measuring two different realities.

* Two separate benchmarks (Userpilot, Amplitude) — different samples, not one funnel. Directional, not peer-reviewed.

Three patterns, over and over

01

Measuring the tour, not the value

A finished checklist is easy to track. A felt value moment is hard to define — so teams default to the easy metric.

Three patterns, over and over

02

Too many steps, too soon

Completion drops from 72% at three steps to just 16% at seven (Chameleon, 2025 Benchmark Report). Every extra step is a chance to lose someone before they've felt any payoff.

Three patterns, over and over

03

One-size-fits-all onboarding

Enterprise products that build real personalization hit 12.4% day-seven activation, versus 2.1% for the median — nearly six times higher, just from not treating every user the same (Amplitude, 2025 Product Benchmark Report).

What great onboarding looks like

Same thread every time: one clear "aha moment," reached as fast as possible.

Slack

The aha moment isn't signing up — it's receiving your first real message from a teammate. Everything before that is just friction to minimize.

Duolingo

The streaks and small wins aren't decoration — they're designed to get you to your first "I can actually do this" moment, before motivation fades.
